## Limitations

- Inventory presence does not prove complete curriculum extraction.

- Missing SAP records have unknown prerequisites; no graph edges are invented.
- Prerequisite expressions are the existing SAP snapshot, not historical rules for each catalog year.
- The catalog contains a trailing unnumbered blank PDF page (380 physical pages, printed through379).
